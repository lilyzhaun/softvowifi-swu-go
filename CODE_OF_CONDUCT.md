# Code of conduct

English and 简体中文 technical contributions are welcome. Treat people respectfully
regardless of background, experience or communication style.

- Discuss code, evidence and tradeoffs; disagree constructively.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate or expose personal data.
- Never post real subscriber identifiers, credentials or private communications.
- Preserve attribution and respect another contributor's existing work.
- Accept corrective feedback and proportionate moderation without retaliation.

These expectations apply to issues, PRs, reviews and other project-managed spaces.
The maintainer may remove inappropriate material or restrict participation to
protect contributors and users.

Use GitHub's **Report content / Report abuse** tools for harassment or private-data
disclosure, rather than a public thread exposing the affected person. Vulnerability
reports use the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). Non-sensitive process
concerns may be raised in an issue without exposing private identities.
